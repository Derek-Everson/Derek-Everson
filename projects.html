My Projects & Experience

Throughout my education and work experience, I have had opportunities to take part in different types of projects and responsibilities. These experiences have helped me understand how planning, teamwork, communication, and consistent effort contribute to completing a successful project.

My background includes customer service and manual labor, as well as academic and personal projects. Each experience has given me an opportunity to learn something new and develop skills that I can carry into future professional environments.

This page highlights the types of work and projects that have contributed to my development.

Work-Based Projects

Whether working with customers, coworkers, equipment, or physical tasks, I have learned how important it is to understand the goal of a task and complete my responsibilities efficiently.

Customer Service Work

Projects and responsibilities involving customer interaction have helped me develop communication, patience, professionalism, and problem-solving skills.

Manual Labor

Research

Gathering information, evaluating material, and developing a better understanding of different subjects.

Organization

Presentation

Developing the ability to communicate information clearly and effectively.
