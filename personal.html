About Me

My name is Derek Everson. I am a student at Iowa State University who is focused on continuing to grow both personally and professionally. My experiences in school, work, leadership, and everyday life have helped me develop a strong sense of responsibility and a willingness to keep learning.

I believe that personal achievement is not limited to awards or formal recognition. The skills and experiences gained through education, employment, leadership, and overcoming challenges are also important parts of personal development.

Educational Achievement

One of my current achievements is continuing my education at Iowa State University. Pursuing a college education requires dedication, organization, and consistent effort.

My academic experience has helped me strengthen my ability to manage deadlines, complete assignments, communicate ideas, and work independently. These skills continue to contribute to my personal development.

I view my education as an important foundation for my future. The knowledge and skills I gain through my coursework can be applied to future professional opportunities and personal goals.

Leadership & Involvement

I am a member of the National Society of Leadership and Success. Being involved in a leadership-focused organization is an important part of my personal development.

Leadership has encouraged me to focus on communication, accountability, teamwork, and continued self-improvement. These are skills that I want to carry with me into both my professional and personal life.

My involvement also reflects my interest in becoming a more well-rounded individual and continuing to develop skills beyond the classroom.

Work Experience

Another important part of my personal development has been gaining experience in different work environments. I have experience in areas including customer service and manual labor.

Working in different environments has helped me understand the importance of reliability, communication, teamwork, and completing responsibilities effectively.

Customer service experience has helped me develop interpersonal and communication skills, while manual labor experience has provided opportunities to develop persistence, responsibility, and practical problem-solving skills.

Skills I Have Developed

Communication

My experiences in school and customer service have helped me develop clearer and more effective communication skills.

Responsibility

Balancing education and work responsibilities has helped me become more organized and accountable.

Teamwork

School, employment, and leadership experiences have provided opportunities to work with others toward shared goals.

Adaptability

Work Ethic

Leadership

My involvement in the National Society of Leadership and Success supports my continued interest in leadership development.

Personal Growth

One of my biggest personal goals is to continue improving. I understand that personal achievement is an ongoing process rather than something that ends after a single accomplishment.

My experiences as a student, employee, and member of a leadership organization have each contributed to my development. I want to continue learning from these experiences and use them to become better prepared for future opportunities.

Looking Toward the Future

As I continue through college and develop my professional experience, I want to continue building on the skills I have already developed.

My goal is to use my education, work experience, and leadership development as a foundation for future opportunities. I am interested in continuing to challenge myself, learn new skills, and take on new responsibilities.
